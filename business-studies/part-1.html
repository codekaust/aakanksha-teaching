Principles and Functions of Management

Business Studies Part 1 · Class XII · CBSE Units 1–8 · 50 of the 80 theory marks

Read this book as one argument, not eight topics

Chapters 1–3 answer what management is, what it is guided by, and where it happens. Chapters 4–8 are then the five functions in the order a manager actually performs them — plan → organise → staff → direct → control — and Chapter 8 closes the loop by handing control's findings straight back to planning. Every chapter opens by re-anchoring in the last one and closes by pointing at the next, so teach them in order.
