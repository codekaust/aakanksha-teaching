LEARNING PYTHON · THE NOTEBOOK PROGRAMS

One program.
One paper run.

Understand what happens at every step.
Then write the program yourself.

A simple daily routine

  1. Trace on paper. Read your assigned program. In your notebook, write its purpose, every step, every variable change and the output. Submit it to your teacher.
  2. Check, then write. Use the password your teacher gives you to check the answer key. Correct your trace, cover the code, then write the program yourself.

Do only the day your teacher assigns. Each answer key has a different password.
